## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is it really free?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. Every feature is free and stays free. <a href="https://streamelements.com/pixieontv/tip">Donations</a> are appreciated but never required.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do you make money if it's free?</b></summary>
<br>
This is a personal project, not a company. It's supported by <a href="https://streamelements.com/pixieontv/tip">donations</a> and by <a href="https://www.pixelychat.com/sponsors.html">sponsors</a>, companies that support development and are shown on the website. No ads in the app, no selling data, and sponsors have no say over the roadmap. Everything the app does today stays free.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does PixelyChat collect or sell my data?</b></summary>
<br>
PixelyChat does not sell your data. Core app data such as settings and stored login tokens stays on your computer. Features that use platform APIs, AI, translation or other network services send only the data needed for that request to the relevant provider. To count how many people use PixelyChat, the app sends one anonymous ping a day with a random install id, your OS, the app version and the app language. No names, accounts or IP addresses are stored. See the <a href="https://www.pixelychat.com/privacy">Privacy Policy</a> for details.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Is it safe to log in with my YouTube/Google account?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. PixelyChat uses the official Google OAuth 2.0 flow. You sign in on Google's own page, and PixelyChat never sees your password.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does PixelyChat support Kick chat?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. Kick is fully supported alongside Twitch, YouTube and TikTok. Kick chat shows up in your unified feed, gets read aloud by TTS, triggers alerts and appears in your overlay exactly like the other platforms.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do the OBS/Streamlabs overlays work?</b></summary>
<br>
PixelyChat runs a small local web server on your own computer. Point a Browser Source at the URL it gives you for chat, alerts, widgets or Companion. No separate cloud overlay service is needed. With OBS Studio you can also connect PixelyChat under Setup → Integrations and add the overlays to your scenes in one click.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I control my stream from my phone?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes, with Pilot. Open the Pilot address shown in PixelyChat in your phone's browser while it's on the same Wi-Fi as your computer. You can read chat, moderate, start Action Widgets, update your title and category, and control OBS.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I read chat while playing with only one monitor?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. Turn on On-Screen Chat under Setup → Overlays. Chat and events appear on top of your game, only you can see them, and your mouse and keyboard stay with the game. It works with windowed and borderless fullscreen games.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I turn Channel Points or TikTok gifts into on-screen rewards?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. Map a Twitch/Kick Channel Point redemption or a specific TikTok gift to its own on-screen reaction: a visual, a sound and optional read-aloud text. Start from bundled presets or upload your own media, and set a point cost per platform.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What is Companion?</b></summary>
<br>
Companion is PixelyChat's optional on-stream AI co-host. It shares the personality selected for AI Chat and reacts with a 2D avatar, voice, captions and event-driven responses. Pick an included avatar, get more from the online library, or create your own. Common reactions use local response libraries, so AI is saved for moments that actually need it.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does PixelyChat work with Stream Deck, Streamer.bot and Discord?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. The PixelyChat plugin for Elgato Stream Deck starts and stops your Action Widgets with one key. Streamer.bot can run your own actions when stream events happen in PixelyChat. Discord Live Sharing posts one announcement with all your live links. All of them are set up under Setup → Integrations.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does it moderate my chat?</b></summary>
<br>
Yes. You can delete messages and block or time out users directly from the unified chat. You can still use your platform's own tools or bots like Nightbot, StreamElements or Streamer.bot alongside it.
</details>

## Who's behind this?

PixelyChat is made by **PixieOnTV**, a variety gaming streamer who got tired of multi-platform chat tools either missing what she needed or requiring a computer science degree to configure. She built it for her own stream first, then figured other streamers fighting the same chat chaos might want it too. Today she builds it together with her community: streamers and viewers who test new versions, report bugs, suggest features, create themes and help with development. [Read the full story →](https://www.pixelychat.com/about.html)
